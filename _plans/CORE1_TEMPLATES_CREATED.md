## 💡 Tips for Filling Templates

- Each template has the **front matter already configured** (title and objective number)
- Templates include placeholder sections for content and related links
- Delete placeholder text and add your content following the guide
- Use headings (`##`, `###`), lists, tables, callouts (`!!! exam`, `!!! tip`, etc.), and images
- Reference the **completed notes** (1.1, 1.2, 2.1, 2.5, 3.1) as examples of formatting

**Example callouts to use:**
```markdown
!!! exam "Exam Focus"
	Key concepts that appear on the CompTIA A+ exam.

!!! tip "Study Tip"
	Memory tricks and practical hints.

!!! danger "Common Mistake"
	Pitfalls to avoid.
```
