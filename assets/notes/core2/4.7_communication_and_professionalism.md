## 📌 Overview

Technicians must demonstrate professionalism, clear communication, cultural awareness, and customer‑focused behavior. Core 2 requires understanding how to interact with customers, manage expectations, avoid distractions, and maintain confidentiality while providing technical support.

---

## 👔 Professional Appearance & Attire

### Present a Professional Appearance
* Clean, neat, and appropriate clothing.
* Maintain good hygiene.
* Avoid distracting accessories.

### Match Required Attire to the Environment
#### Formal
* Suits, dress shirts, ties.
* Required in executive or high‑visibility environments.

#### Business Casual
* Collared shirts, slacks, modest footwear.
* Common in most IT departments.

---

## 🗣 Proper Language & Communication

### Use Proper Language
* Avoid:
  * Jargon  
  * Acronyms  
  * Slang  
* Use clear, simple explanations.

### Maintain a Positive Attitude & Project Confidence
* Stay calm and reassuring.
* Avoid sounding uncertain or frustrated.

### Actively Listen
* Allow customer to finish speaking.
* Use verbal cues (“I understand,” “Go on”).
* Take notes to avoid missing details.

---

## 🌍 Cultural Sensitivity

### Use Appropriate Professional Titles
* Examples:
  * Mr. / Ms. / Dr.  
  * Job titles (Manager, Director)
* Avoid assumptions about names or roles.

### Respect Cultural Differences
* Be mindful of communication styles.
* Avoid humor or idioms that may not translate well.

---

## ⏰ Punctuality & Reliability

### Be On Time
* Arrive at scheduled appointments promptly.
* If running late:
  * Contact customer immediately  
  * Provide updated ETA  

---

## 📵 Avoiding Distractions

### Personal Calls
* Silence phone during support interactions.

### Texting / Social Media
* Never use personal devices while assisting customers.

### Personal Interruptions
* Avoid stepping away unless absolutely necessary.

---

## 😣 Handling Difficult Customers & Situations

### Do Not Argue or Become Defensive
* Stay calm and professional.
* Focus on resolving the issue.

### Avoid Dismissing Customer Issues
* Even small problems matter to the customer.
* Validate their concerns.

### Avoid Being Judgmental
* Never criticize user mistakes or lack of knowledge.

### Clarify Customer Statements
* Use open‑ended questions:
  * “Can you describe what happened before the error?”
  * “What were you trying to do when the issue occurred?”
* Restate the issue to confirm understanding.

### Use Discretion & Professionalism
* Avoid sharing:
  * Personal opinions  
  * Stories about other customers  
  * Sensitive experiences  

---

## 📅 Setting & Meeting Expectations

### Communicate Timelines
* Provide realistic estimates.
* Update customer if delays occur.

### Offer Repair / Replacement Options
* Present choices clearly.
* Explain pros and cons.

### Provide Proper Documentation
* Include:
  * Work performed  
  * Parts replaced  
  * Recommendations  
  * Ticket notes  

### Follow Up Later
* Verify customer satisfaction.
* Ensure issue remains resolved.

---

## 🔒 Handling Confidential & Private Materials

* Protect customer data found on:
  * Computers  
  * Desktops  
  * Printers  
  * External drives  
* Never:
  * Read personal files  
  * Copy data unnecessarily  
  * Discuss private information  
* Follow company privacy policies.

---

## ⚡ Key Vocabulary

Active Listening  
: Fully focusing on the speaker without interrupting.

Open-Ended Questions  
: Questions that encourage detailed responses.

Professionalism  
: Conduct that reflects respect, competence, and reliability.

Confidentiality  
: Protecting private customer information from unauthorized access.

---

## ⚡ Exam Tips & Pitfalls

!!! exam "Exam Tip: Communication"
    CompTIA heavily tests **active listening**, avoiding jargon, and maintaining professionalism.

!!! danger "Common Pitfall: Arguing with Customers"
    Never argue — redirect conversation toward solutions.

!!! tip "Remember: Expectations"
    Always set clear timelines and follow up after service.

!!! warning "Watch Out: Confidential Data"
    Technicians must protect customer privacy at all times.

!!! example "Scenario: Upset Customer"
    **Scenario:** A customer is frustrated and raising their voice.  
    **Solution:** Stay calm, avoid arguing, acknowledge their concern, clarify the issue, and offer solutions.
