## 📌 Overview

Technicians must understand how to handle prohibited content, preserve evidence, respect licensing agreements, protect regulated data, and follow organizational policies. Core 2 requires knowing incident‑response steps, privacy requirements, licensing types, and compliance expectations.

---

## 🚨 Incident Response

Incident response ensures evidence is preserved, legal requirements are met, and security breaches are handled properly.

### Chain of Custody
* Documents **who handled evidence**, when, and why.
* Ensures integrity for legal or disciplinary action.
* Applies to:
  * Hard drives  
  * Logs  
  * Screenshots  
  * Devices containing prohibited content  

### Informing Management / Law Enforcement
* Required when:
  * Illegal content is found  
  * Threats or harassment occur  
  * Major security breaches happen  
* Technicians **must not investigate beyond their role** — escalate immediately.

### Copy of Drive (Data Integrity & Preservation)
* Create a **forensic image** of the drive.
* Prevents altering original evidence.
* Use write blockers when possible.

### Incident Documentation
* Record:
  * Time of discovery  
  * Who reported the issue  
  * Actions taken  
  * Evidence collected  
* Must be clear, factual, and complete.

### Order of Volatility
* Collect evidence from **most volatile to least**:
  1. CPU registers/cache  
  2. RAM  
  3. Network connections  
  4. Running processes  
  5. Disk data  
  6. Backups  
* Helps preserve data that disappears quickly.

## 🔑 Licensing, DRM & EULA Concepts

### Valid Licenses
* Software must be properly licensed.
* Unauthorized use violates:
  * Company policy  
  * Copyright law  

### Perpetual License Agreement
* One-time purchase.
* User can use software indefinitely (version-specific).

### Personal-Use vs. Corporate-Use License
* Personal-use:
  * Intended for individual consumer use.
  * Not valid for business environments.
* Corporate-use:
  * Includes business rights, support, and compliance.

### Open-Source License
* Source code available for modification.
* Examples:
  * GPL  
  * MIT  
  * Apache  
* Still requires compliance with license terms.

### Digital Rights Management (DRM)
* Controls access to digital content.
* Prevents unauthorized copying or distribution.

---

## 🤐 Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDA / MNDA)

* Legal agreements preventing disclosure of sensitive information.
* MNDA = mutual NDA (both parties restricted).
* Common for:
  * Contractors  
  * Vendors  
  * Employees handling confidential data

## 🔒 Regulated Data

Technicians must protect regulated data according to legal and industry standards.

### Credit Card Payment Information
* Governed by **PCI-DSS**.
* Requires:
  * Encryption  
  * Restricted access  
  * Secure storage  

### Personal Government-Issued Information
* Includes:
  * SSN  
  * Driver’s license numbers  
  * Passport numbers  
* Must be stored securely and accessed only when necessary.

### Personally Identifiable Information (PII)
* Any data that identifies an individual.
* Examples:
  * Name  
  * Address  
  * Email  
  * Phone number  

### Healthcare Data
* Governed by **HIPAA**.
* Requires:
  * Strict access controls  
  * Audit logs  
  * Encryption  

### Data Retention Requirements
* Laws dictate how long data must be stored.
* Examples:
  * Financial records  
  * Medical records  
  * Employee files

## 📜 Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

* Defines what users **can and cannot do** on company systems.
* Covers:
  * Internet usage  
  * Email usage  
  * Prohibited content  
  * Security expectations  
* Violations may result in disciplinary action.

---

## 🏛 Regulatory & Business Compliance Requirements

### Splash Screens
* Display:
  * Legal notices  
  * Security warnings  
  * AUP reminders  
* Reinforce compliance and user accountability.

## ⚡ Key Vocabulary

Chain of Custody  
: Documentation showing who handled evidence and when.

Order of Volatility  
: Priority list for collecting evidence based on how quickly it disappears.

EULA  
: Agreement defining how software may be used.

PII  
: Data that identifies an individual.

AUP  
: Policy defining acceptable use of company systems.

## ⚡ Exam Tips & Pitfalls

!!! exam "Exam Tip: Chain of Custody"
    If evidence is involved, **chain of custody** must be documented immediately.

!!! danger "Common Pitfall: Investigating Illegal Content"
    Technicians must **stop and escalate** — never attempt to analyze illegal content themselves.

!!! tip "Remember: Licensing"
    Personal-use licenses are **not valid** for corporate environments.

!!! warning "Watch Out: Regulated Data"
    Healthcare = HIPAA.  
    Credit cards = PCI-DSS.  
    Government IDs = strict privacy laws.

!!! example "Scenario: Technician Finds Illegal Images"
    **Scenario:** A technician discovers prohibited content on a user’s PC.  
    **Solution:** Stop work, document the incident, preserve evidence, notify management, and follow chain-of-custody procedures.
