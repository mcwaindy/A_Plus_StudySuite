## 📌 Overview

Artificial intelligence (AI) is increasingly integrated into business workflows, applications, and support systems. Core 2 requires understanding how AI is used, the policies governing its use, its limitations, and the differences between private and public AI systems.

---

## 🔗 Application Integration

AI can be embedded into various tools and workflows to improve efficiency and decision-making.

### Common Integration Areas
* **Productivity tools**
  * Email drafting  
  * Document summarization  
  * Scheduling assistance  

* **Support systems**
  * Automated troubleshooting  
  * Knowledge-base search  
  * Ticket classification  

* **Business applications**
  * Data analysis  
  * Pattern recognition  
  * Predictive modeling  

* **Security tools**
  * Threat detection  
  * Log analysis  
  * Behavioral monitoring  

---

## 📜 Policy Considerations

Organizations must define clear policies for how AI can be used.

### Appropriate Use
* Use AI tools only for approved business tasks.
* Avoid using AI to:
  * Generate harmful content  
  * Circumvent security controls  
  * Access unauthorized data  

### Plagiarism
* AI-generated content may resemble existing material.
* Users must:
  * Verify originality  
  * Cite sources when required  
  * Avoid submitting AI-generated work as personal creation when prohibited  

---

## ⚠️ Limitations of AI

AI is powerful but not perfect. Understanding its limitations prevents misuse and incorrect assumptions.

### Bias
* AI models may reflect biases present in training data.
* Can lead to:
  * Skewed recommendations  
  * Unfair outputs  
  * Misinterpretation of user intent  

### Hallucinations
* AI may generate incorrect or fabricated information.
* Users must verify accuracy before relying on outputs.

### Accuracy
* AI responses depend on:
  * Training data quality  
  * Context clarity  
  * Model limitations  
* Always validate critical information.

---

## 🔒 Private vs. Public AI

AI systems differ in how they handle data, privacy, and security.

### Data Security
* **Private AI**
  * Often runs within an organization’s environment.
  * Provides stronger control over data access and retention.
* **Public AI**
  * Hosted externally.
  * Requires careful review of data-sharing policies.

### Data Source
* Private AI may use:
  * Internal documents  
  * Company knowledge bases  
* Public AI uses:
  * Broad internet data  
  * Public datasets  

### Data Privacy
* Private AI:
  * Designed to protect sensitive business information.
  * Often complies with internal privacy standards.
* Public AI:
  * Users must avoid entering confidential or regulated data unless permitted.

---

## ⚡ Key Vocabulary

Bias  
: Systematic skew in AI outputs caused by training data.

Hallucination  
: AI-generated information that is incorrect or fabricated.

Appropriate Use  
: Following organizational rules for how AI tools may be used.

Private AI  
: AI system operating within a controlled business environment.

## ⚡ Exam Tips & Pitfalls

!!! exam "Exam Tip: AI Limitations"
    If a scenario mentions incorrect or fabricated responses, the correct concept is **hallucination**.

!!! danger "Common Pitfall: Entering Sensitive Data"
    Public AI tools should **not** be used for regulated or confidential information.

!!! tip "Remember: Bias"
    AI can unintentionally reinforce patterns from its training data — always verify outputs.

!!! warning "Watch Out: Plagiarism"
    AI-generated content may resemble existing work; users must follow organizational policies.

!!! example "Scenario: AI Suggests Incorrect Troubleshooting Steps"
    **Scenario:** A technician uses an AI tool and receives inaccurate instructions.  
    **Solution:** Recognize this as an AI **hallucination**, verify information manually, and follow proper procedures.
