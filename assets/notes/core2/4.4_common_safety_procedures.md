## 📌 Overview

Safety procedures protect technicians, equipment, and work environments from electrical hazards, physical injury, and component damage. Core 2 requires understanding ESD protection, proper handling techniques, personal safety gear, and compliance with regulations.

---

## ⚡ Electrostatic Discharge (ESD) Protection

### ESD Straps
* Worn on the wrist and connected to a grounded surface.
* Prevents static discharge from damaging sensitive components.
* Must maintain skin contact for proper grounding.

### ESD Mats
* Provides a grounded workspace.
* Used with ESD straps for full protection.
* Prevents static buildup on tools and components.

---

## 🔌 Electrical Safety

### Equipment Grounding
* Ensures electrical faults safely discharge to ground.
* Prevents electric shock and equipment damage.
* Includes:
  * Grounded outlets  
  * Properly grounded power supplies  
  * Avoiding use of damaged cables  

---

## 📦 Proper Component Handling & Storage

* Hold components by edges — avoid touching circuitry.
* Store components in:
  * Antistatic bags  
  * ESD-safe containers  
* Avoid stacking heavy items on sensitive hardware.
* Keep liquids away from work areas.

---

## 🧵 Cable Management

* Prevents tripping hazards.
* Reduces strain on connectors.
* Improves airflow inside PCs.
* Use:
  * Velcro straps  
  * Cable ties  
  * Routing channels  

---

## 🛍 Antistatic Bags

* Used for storing:
  * RAM  
  * GPUs  
  * Motherboards  
  * SSDs/HDDs  
* Prevent static discharge during transport or storage.

---

## 🏛 Compliance with Government Regulations

* Follow OSHA guidelines for workplace safety.
* Proper disposal of:
  * Batteries  
  * Electronics  
  * Hazardous materials  
* Ensure fire extinguishers and first-aid kits meet regulatory standards.

---

## 🧑‍🔧 Personal Safety

### Disconnect Power Before Repairing PC
* Prevents electrical shock.
* Avoids short circuits.
* Always unplug power supply before opening case.

### Lifting Techniques
* Bend knees, not back.
* Use team lifting for heavy servers or UPS units.
* Prevents back injuries.

### Fire Safety
* Know extinguisher types:
  * **Class C** for electrical fires.
* Keep exits clear.
* Avoid overloading power strips.

### Safety Goggles
* Protect eyes from:
  * Debris  
  * Metal shavings  
  * Accidental tool slips  

### Air Filter Mask
* Used when:
  * Cleaning dusty PCs  
  * Working in poorly ventilated areas  
* Prevents inhalation of dust and particulates.

---

## ⚡ Key Vocabulary

ESD  
: Static electricity discharge that can damage components.

Grounding  
: Connecting equipment to earth to prevent electrical shock.

Antistatic Bag  
: Protective packaging preventing static buildup.

Class C Extinguisher  
: Fire extinguisher designed for electrical fires.

---

## ⚡ Exam Tips & Pitfalls

!!! exam "Exam Tip: ESD"
    ESD straps must be connected to **ground**, not the PC case alone.

!!! danger "Common Pitfall: Forgetting to Disconnect Power"
    Always unplug the PC before opening it — even if the power switch is off.

!!! tip "Remember: Fire Extinguishers"
    Electrical fires require **Class C** extinguishers.

!!! warning "Watch Out: Component Handling"
    Touching circuitry can transfer oils or static — always hold components by the edges.

!!! example "Scenario: Technician Gets Shocked"
    **Scenario:** A technician receives a mild shock while working inside a PC.  
    **Solution:** Ensure proper grounding, disconnect power, inspect cables, and follow electrical safety procedures.
