## 📌 Overview

Connecting internal PC components, external peripherals, displays, and power supplies requires an understanding of distinct cable standards, pin counts, bus speeds, and signal types (analog vs. digital). This objective covers internal power connectors, peripheral data cables, video display interfaces, serial connections, and common signal adapters.

---

## ⚡ Internal Power Connectors

Power Supply Units (PSUs) convert AC power from wall outlets into DC voltages (+3.3V, +5V, +12V) delivered to motherboard circuits and internal components via specialized keyed connectors.

### Main Motherboard Power
* **24-Pin ATX Power Connector:** The primary power connection for modern motherboards. Delivers +3.3V, +5V, and +12V power rails. Many PSUs use a **20+4 pin** split connector design to remain backward-compatible with legacy 20-pin ATX motherboards.

### Supplemental CPU & PCIe Power
* **4/8-Pin EPS / ATX12V Power:** Dedicated connector that delivers direct +12V power specifically to the CPU power regulation circuits (VRMs) on the motherboard. Often splits into two 4-pin halves (4+4 pin).
* **6/8-Pin PCIe Power:** Dedicated connector designed to deliver extra +12V power directly to high-performance dedicated Expansion Video Cards (GPUs). Standard 6-pin delivers up to 75W; 8-pin (often 6+2 pin) delivers up to 150W.

### Storage & Legacy Component Power
* **SATA Power Connector:** 15-pin L-shaped connector used to power 2.5-inch/3.5-inch internal SATA hard drives, SSDs, and optical drives. Supplies +3.3V, +5V, and +12V.
* **Molex (4-Pin Peripheral):** Legacy 4-pin splayed connector used to power older IDE hard drives, optical drives, case fans, and pump accessories. Delivers +5V (red wire) and +12V (yellow wire).

## 🔌 Peripheral & Storage Data Cables

Data buses transport digital signals between internal storage drives, system motherboards, and external peripherals.

### USB (Universal Serial Bus) Connectors
* **USB-A:** Standard rectangular 4-pin connector found on PCs, laptops, and chargers. Non-reversible (keyed).
* **USB-B:** Square connector with chamfered upper corners commonly used on larger peripheral devices like printers, scanners, and audio interfaces.
* **USB-C:** Compact 24-pin reversible connector supporting high-speed data (USB 3.2 / USB4), DisplayPort video output, and high-wattage power delivery (USB-PD up to 240W).
* **Micro-USB:** Small 5-pin trapezoidal connector previously standard on mobile phones, power banks, and portable peripherals.
* **Mini-USB:** Older, thicker 5-pin trapezoidal connector used on legacy digital cameras, GPS units, and early external hard drives.

### Storage Data Cables
* **SATA Data Cable:** 7-pin narrow ribbon cable used to connect internal SATA HDDs, SSDs, and optical drives to motherboard SATA ports. Max speed is **6 Gbps (SATA Revision 3.0)**.
* **eSATA (External SATA):** 7-pin shielded external data cable for attaching external hard drives at full internal SATA bus speeds. Features non-L-shaped keying to prevent accidental insertion of internal SATA cables.

### Legacy Serial / Console
* **DB-9 / RS-232:** 9-pin D-subminiature connector used for serial communications. Primarily used by network technicians (via a console/rollover cable) to establish direct command-line management connections to routers, switches, and industrial equipment.

## 📺 Video Display Interfaces

Display connectors transmit analog or digital audio and video signals from computer graphics cards to external monitors and projectors.

| Video Standard | Signal Type | Audio Support | Max Distance / Key Feature |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **VGA (DE-15)** | **Analog Only** | No | 15-pin 3-row blue connector; susceptible to signal degradation |
| **DVI-A** | Analog Only | No | Transmits legacy analog RGB signals only |
| **DVI-D** | Digital Only | No | Single-link or Dual-link digital video transmission |
| **DVI-I** | Analog & Digital | No | Integrated connector carrying both analog and digital pins |
| **HDMI** | **Digital Only** | **Yes** (Audio + Video) | 19-pin consumer standard; carries uncompressed video & multi-channel audio |
| **DisplayPort** | **Digital Only** | **Yes** (Audio + Video) | 20-pin packet-based interface; features physical locking latch mechanism |

## 🔄 Adapters & Signal Converters

When connecting equipment with mismatched video or data interfaces, passive adapters or active converters bridge the hardware gap.

* **HDMI to VGA Adapter:** An **active converter** that translates digital HDMI video signals into analog VGA RGB signals. Requires internal processing chips because digital and analog are electrically incompatible.
* **DVI to HDMI Adapter:** A **passive adapter** because DVI-D/DVI-I digital signals are electrically compatible with HDMI video signals (no signal translation processing required). Note that standard DVI does not carry audio.
* **USB to Ethernet Adapter:** Converts an open USB port (USB-A or USB-C) into an RJ-45 Gigabit Ethernet network port for laptops lacking a built-in NIC.
* **USB to Wi-Fi Adapter:** Compact USB dongle adding wireless 802.11 network connectivity to desktop PCs or devices with failed internal wireless cards.

## ⚡ Key Vocabulary

20+4 Pin ATX
: The primary 24-pin motherboard power connector designed with a detachable 4-pin segment for backward compatibility with 20-pin motherboards.

Active Converter
: An adapter containing integrated circuit chips to translate incompatible signal types (e.g., Digital HDMI to Analog VGA).

Passive Adapter
: A simple pin-to-pin mechanical adapter connecting two electrically compatible interfaces without processing signals (e.g., DVI-D to HDMI).

Molex
: A 4-pin peripheral power connector used for older internal components, delivering +5V and +12V.

## ⚡ Exam Tips & Pitfalls

!!! exam "Exam Tip: Passive vs. Active Video Adapters"
    * **DVI-D to HDMI:** **Passive** adapter (both use digital video signaling).
    * **HDMI to VGA:** **Active** converter required (converts Digital $\rightarrow$ Analog). Passive wire adapters will not work!

!!! danger "Common Pitfall: CPU 8-Pin vs. PCIe 8-Pin"
    Do not confuse the **8-pin EPS CPU power connector** (4+4 pin split) with the **8-pin PCIe graphics card connector** (6+2 pin split)! Forcing a PCIe cable into the CPU motherboard header will short-circuit the board due to reversed +12V and Ground pin assignments.

!!! tip "Remember: DisplayPort Locking Latch"
    Unlike HDMI, standard **DisplayPort connectors** feature physical spring-loaded locking hooks. Always squeeze the release button on the connector housing before pulling a DisplayPort cable out of a monitor or GPU!

!!! warning "Watch Out: DVI Pin Variations"
    * **DVI-D:** Digital only (Single/Dual link). Flat blade pin without surrounding 4 pins.
    * **DVI-A:** Analog only.
    * **DVI-I:** Integrated Digital and Analog (Flat blade pin surrounded by 4 analog pins).

!!! example "Scenario: Connecting a Modern Laptop to an Older Projector"
    **Scenario:** A presenter brings a modern ultra-thin laptop (USB-C and HDMI ports only) to give a presentation, but the conference room projector only accepts 15-pin VGA analog inputs.
    
    **Solution:** Connect an **HDMI-to-VGA active adapter** or a **USB-C to VGA display adapter** to convert the laptop's digital output into an analog video signal for the projector.
