## How to Study
1. Click "Flash Cards" in sidebar
2. Select objective from dropdown
3. Click card to flip (term ↔ definition)
4. Toggle "Start with Definition" if desired
5. Mark each card as "Know It" or "Needs Review"
6. Switch objectives anytime to study different domains
